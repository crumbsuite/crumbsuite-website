Delete Your Crumb Suite Account

At Crumb Suite, we respect your privacy and give users full control over their account and data.

Users can request account deletion directly from within the Crumb Suite Android app.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Crumb Suite app on your Android phone.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Read the confirmation message carefully.
  5. Confirm your request to delete your account and associated data.
Once confirmed, your account deletion request cannot be reversed.

What Data Will Be Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data associated with your account may be removed:

  • Account profile information
  • Business or bakery details
  • Orders and sales records
  • Inventory and stock records
  • Recipes and costing details
  • Production planning records
  • Reports and analysis data
  • Cloud backup data linked to your account

Data Retention

After you request account deletion, your account and associated data will be deleted from our active systems within a reasonable processing period.

Some limited data may be retained temporarily if required for security, legal, fraud prevention, tax, dispute resolution, or compliance purposes.
