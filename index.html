Features

Everything Your Bakery Needs

From the first order of the day to the final summary at night, CrumbSuite keeps every part of your business in sync.

📋

Orders & Clients

Create and manage orders end-to-end — draft, confirmed, delivered. Track partial payments and follow up on pending balances with ease.

  • Full order lifecycle management
  • Client profiles with history
  • Partial & full payment tracking
  • Reminders for upcoming deliveries
🧁

Product Catalog

Organise your full range of baked goods with pricing, categories and beautiful product cards.

📖

Recipe Builder

Build recipes with ingredient-level costing so you always know your margins before you bake.

📦

Inventory & Stock

Track raw materials, get low-stock alerts and auto-deduct ingredients when production runs.

📊

Reports & Insights

Daily summaries, revenue charts and business KPIs at a glance — no spreadsheets required.

👥

Team & Roles

Invite staff and admins to your bakery workspace. Role-based permissions ensure everyone sees only what they need to.

  • Owner, Admin and Staff roles
  • Invite via email
  • Least-privilege access control
  • Member activity tracking
How It Works

Up and Running in Minutes

No training required. CrumbSuite is designed to be picked up by any baker on day one.

01

Create Your Bakery

Sign up, enter your bakery name and you're in. Your private workspace is set up instantly with no extra forms.

02

Add Products & Recipes

Build your product catalog and link each item to a recipe with costing — so you always bake at the right price.

03

Take Orders & Get Paid

Start taking orders, mark payments and track outstanding balances — all from one place on your phone.

04

Review & Grow

Check daily summaries, spot your best-sellers and use insights to make smarter decisions every day.
