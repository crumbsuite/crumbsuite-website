Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CrumbSuite collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile app and website.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account details such as your name, email address, and bakery name.
  • Business data you enter in the app, including orders, products, stock, recipes, and payment records.
  • Basic usage and device information needed to run, maintain, and improve the service.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide app features and save your business data securely.
  • To improve reliability, performance, and user experience.
  • To communicate important service updates and respond to support requests.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only with trusted service providers who help us operate CrumbSuite (for example, hosting and infrastructure), and only as needed for service delivery.

4. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we continuously work to protect your data.

5. Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

6. Your Choices

  • You can request account-related updates or deletion by contacting us.
  • You may stop using the service at any time.

7. Children

CrumbSuite is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact us at crumbsuite@gmail.com.
